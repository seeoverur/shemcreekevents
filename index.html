Welcome to the Creek

About Shem Creek

Shem Creek is a tidal waterway that flows through Mount Pleasant and opens into Charleston Harbor. Its waterfront district combines a working commercial fishing creek with restaurants, bars, public boardwalks, marinas and recreational businesses.

Visitors come to watch shrimp boats, walk beside the marsh, photograph sunsets, look for dolphins and enjoy seafood beside the water. Kayaks, paddleboards, sailboats, charter boats and fishing vessels regularly travel through the creek.

The area can become busy during evenings, weekends and warm-weather events. Parking is limited in several sections, so visitors should arrive early, use designated public parking and follow all posted parking restrictions.

Free Waterfront Attraction

Shem Creek Park and Boardwalk

The public boardwalk is one of the easiest ways to experience the creek without making a restaurant reservation or booking a boat.

Visitors can walk through the salt marsh, observe working shrimp docks, watch boats pass through the creek and look toward Charleston Harbor. The park provides a popular setting for casual walks, wildlife viewing, fishing and sunset photography.

  • Public walking trail and boardwalk
  • Marsh and Charleston Harbor views
  • Fishing access
  • ADA-accessible areas
  • Public parking and restrooms

The Working Waterfront

A Shem Creek seafood business connected to generations of local shrimping and the Magwood family’s fishing heritage.

A commercial fishing operation known for supplying locally caught fish to area restaurants and participating customers.

A family seafood operation associated with Shem Creek’s active commercial fishing community.

Geechie Seafood

A wholesale and retail seafood business connected to the creek’s long shrimping history.

Local Shrimp Boats

Commercial vessels continue to operate from the creek. Visitors should observe docks from public areas and avoid entering active work zones.

Seafood selection and direct-to-public availability depend on the season, weather, fishing conditions and individual business schedules.

Visitor Information

Planning a Day at Shem Creek

Build your visit around the time of day, expected crowds and the type of creek experience you want.

Morning

Walk the boardwalk, take an early kayak tour or visit Mill Street Tavern for breakfast or brunch before larger crowds arrive.

Afternoon

Rent a boat or paddleboard, shop for fresh seafood and stop for a casual waterfront lunch.

Evening

Reserve a waterfront dinner, book a sunset cruise or arrive early to find a good view from a restaurant deck.

Parking

Use public spaces and obey signs. Do not park in reserved restaurant, hotel, marina or business spaces unless you are a customer.

Water conditions, tides, heat and thunderstorms can affect paddling and boating. Follow operator instructions and local safety rules when participating in water activities.
