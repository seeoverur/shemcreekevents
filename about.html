About Shem Creek Events

Shem Creek Events is an independent reference guide covering restaurants, waterfront businesses, outdoor activities and happenings around Shem Creek in Mount Pleasant, South Carolina.

Our Local Reference Guide

Discover Shem Creek in 2026

Shem Creek is one of Mount Pleasant’s most recognizable waterfront destinations. The area combines a working commercial fishing creek with seafood restaurants, outdoor bars, hotels, marinas, public boardwalks and water recreation businesses.

Shem Creek Events was created to give residents and visitors a convenient starting point for learning about the area. Our guide highlights places to eat, businesses to visit, boat tours, kayak rentals, live music, seasonal gatherings and other ways to experience the creek.

The goal of this website is to maintain a useful Shem Creek reference that reflects information available in 2026. Because restaurant hours, event dates, tour schedules and business offerings can change, visitors should confirm important details before making reservations or traveling to the creek.

2026 Visitor Information

Shem Creek Park continues to provide public access to the waterfront, including walking areas, fishing access, parking and restrooms. The boardwalk offers views of the creek, surrounding marsh and Charleston Harbor.

Parking can become limited during evenings, weekends and special events. Visitors should use designated spaces and avoid parking in areas reserved for restaurants, hotels, marinas or private businesses.
